GAMEPLAY EXPERIMENT / SUMIT PAUL

Hold on.
Against the pull.

Black-hole physics · Grabbing & stamina

A black hole pulls surrounding objects inward. Sumit developed a grabbing mechanic that lets the player hold on against the pull, with repeated key presses needed to maintain their grip and stamina.

The experiment brings physics, player input, and resource management together in a struggle to keep hold.
